Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Hum kaun hain

Expo Connect ek website hai jo expos aur trade fairs ki event staffing jobs dikhati hai aur candidates ko un jobs se jodti hai.

2. Hum kaunsi jaankari lete hain

  • Naam, WhatsApp/phone number, email (optional), age
  • Login ke liye 6 ank ka PIN (hum use surakshit hash ke roop mein rakhte hain, asli PIN nahi)
  • Role, experience, preferred venues aur availability
  • Aapne kis job par apply kiya, aur uska status

3. Kyun lete hain

Aapki jaankari ka use sirf event jobs ke liye aapko shortlist ya select karne, WhatsApp, phone ya email par sampark karne, aur duty aur payment se jude updates dene ke liye hota hai.

4. Kisi ke saath share hoti hai

Hum aapki jaankari bechte nahi hain. Selection ke liye zaroorat ho to event ke organizer ya client ke saath zaroori jaankari (jaise naam, phone aur role) share ho sakti hai. Data Supabase ke servers par store hota hai aur website Render par chalti hai.

5. Kitne samay tak rakhte hain

Jab tak aap active candidate hain ya jab tak aap delete karne ko nahi kehte.

6. Aapke adhikaar

Aap apni jaankari dekhne, sudhaarne ya delete karwane ko keh sakte hain, aur apni sehmati wapas le sakte hain. Iske liye website par diye gaye WhatsApp Community link ke zariye hamse sampark karo.

7. Suraksha

Hum aapki jaankari ki suraksha ke liye zaroori kadam uthate hain, lekin koi bhi online system 100% surakshit nahi hota.

8. Umar

Ye website sirf 18 saal ya usse upar ke candidates ke liye hai.

9. Badlav

Ye policy kabhi bhi badal sakti hai. Naya version isi page par dikhega.
